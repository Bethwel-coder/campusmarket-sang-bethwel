Buy and sell with people you share a campus with.

CampusMarket is a small online marketplace for students and staff. Find textbooks, hostel essentials and gadgets from your university community, often just a lecture hall away.

Browse the catalog

What you can find

Books and study gear

Second-hand textbooks, calculators and lab supplies at student-friendly prices.

Hostel essentials

Lamps, kettles and storage for your room, passed on by students who have finished their studies.

Electronics

Earbuds, chargers and accessories that make studying and commuting easier.

How CampusMarket works

  1. Browse

    Look through the catalog to see what is available.

  2. Choose

    Check the price and description of each item.

  3. Pick up on campus

    Meet on campus and collect your item.
